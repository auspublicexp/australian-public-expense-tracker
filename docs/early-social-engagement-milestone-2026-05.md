# Early APET social engagement milestone — 21 May 2026

On 21 May 2026, an APET X notification showed **Radio Australis (@freedom4UU)** quoting an APET chart post, and a separate notification showed the account liking the APET post.

The chart was titled **“Top Domestic Travel — Jan–Mar 2024”**, showing selected parliamentarians' reported domestic travel expenditure according to IPEA parliamentary expenditure data. The original APET caption identified the reporting period and official source.

The quoting account added its own political/economic commentary about struggling families and unemployment. **That commentary was the third party's interpretation, not an APET finding or endorsement.** The milestone illustrates how a neutral official-data visual can circulate among audiences that draw their own conclusions.

## Lessons retained

- Maintain fact-first captions with the reporting period, expenditure definition and official source.
- Let others interpret a chart without automatically entering political discussions.
- Acknowledge appropriate quotes or reposts with a like if the surrounding commentary does not undermine APET's neutral stance.
- Reply only to clarify a factual error, source, definition or methodological limitation.
- Treat organic sharing as an encouraging qualitative signal, not as evidence of long-term reach or impact.

## Evidence and limits

The source for this note is a contemporaneous X notifications screenshot shared in the APET project chat on 21 May 2026. The screenshot is **not archived in this repository**. It confirms the visible notifications at that moment, but not the quoted post's long-term availability, total reach, or subsequent outcomes. No original post URL was recorded.
